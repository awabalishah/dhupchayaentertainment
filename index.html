    About Us

    ধূপছায়া — light & shade

    Dhupchaya is the play of sunlight and shadow. It's also how we think about stories: the warm moments and the dark ones, side by side.

    Dhupchaya Entertainment produces and releases Bangla natoks, YouTube films, short films and original music. We work with the country's best-loved actors, Apurba, Tasnia Farin, Musfiq R Farhan, Tanjin Tisha and Arosh Khan among them, and with directors who know how to hold an audience for an hour on a phone screen.

    Our mission is to be the most original, versatile media production house in Bangla entertainment, known for its storytelling and its creative concepts.
